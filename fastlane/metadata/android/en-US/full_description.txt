Turn your real life into a role-playing game. Life RPG lets you define your own quests, complete them, and watch your hero grow as you build better habits.

<b>Core gameplay</b>
* Create quests with 4 difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Legendary
* Earn XP, coins and climb the ranks as you complete your tasks
* Build daily streaks and unlock streak milestones
* Level up your hero and unlock new titles

<b>Your character</b>
* Customize your hero: hair, eyes, mouth, outfits, hats and accessories
* See your character evolve as your hero progresses

<b>Stay motivated</b>
* Android home screen widgets: current quest, hero progress, quick add, daily quests and a monthly calendar
* Notifications and daily reminders to keep your habits on track
* A monthly calendar to visualize your tasks

<b>Deep gameplay systems</b>
* A skill tree to specialize your hero
* A shop to spend your coins
* Guilds, a leaderboard and referral codes to play with friends
* Optional bets to add stakes to your habits
* Weekly summaries and statistics to follow your progress

<b>Privacy first</b>
* Everything is stored locally on your device
* Optional synchronization with your own Life RPG server (self-hosted)
* No ads, no trackers, no account required

Life RPG is free and open source software (GPL-3.0).
